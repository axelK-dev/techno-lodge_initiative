# Rollout & Governance

A **phased rollout** and **participatory governance** model ensure equity and local ownership with a consistent backbone.

## Phases (Illustrative)
1. **Tribal‑first pilots** — Co‑designed sites with FPIC (Free, Prior, Informed Consent), commissioned art, language modules, digital literacy & cybersecurity training.
2. **Rural expansion** — Community anchor lodges aligned with broadband office goals.
3. **Urban hubs** — Advanced tech modules; continuous feedback and equity dashboards.

## Governance Layers
- **Statewide Steering Council (SSC)** — Direction, funding, standards, equity KPIs.
- **Tribal Governance Forum (TGF)** — Government‑to‑government consultation; FPIC authority for cultural modules, siting, and data.
- **Regional Advisory Boards (RABs)** — Local planning, module selection, listening sessions.
- **Site Governance Teams (SGTs)** — Daily operations; Accessibility Lead; Youth & Elders Councils input.

## Roles & Gamification
- **Explorer → Builder → Mentor → Innovator** progression tied to contributions.
- Badges/leaderboards foster engagement; internships unlocked at higher roles.

## Risk & Mitigation (Examples)
- **Connectivity delays** → interim fixed wireless/satellite; keep programming.
- **Cultural disputes** → TGF mediation; pause cultural modules until consent.
- **Capacity** → fund staff time for consultation and reporting.

**Cultural Design Disclaimer**

Any Indigenous art or cultural elements referenced in this initiative will only be used with explicit permission from the relevant Tribes and artists. The current visuals are conceptual and do not include authentic tribal designs. Before incorporating cultural motifs in production assets or spaces, the project will:

- Conduct government-to-government consultation with the appropriate Tribes.
- Obtain Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC).
- Commission Indigenous artists and cultural advisors for authentic representation.
- Provide interpretive context for any cultural elements included.

These steps ensure respect, authenticity, and compliance with cultural protocols.
